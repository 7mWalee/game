HOW TO PLAY

Siren Head levelled Hollow Creek. Nobody believes you. Photograph 4 pieces of evidence and the police will send in the heavy equipment.

He is blind. He hunts by sound. Running, jumping, panting and even the buzz of your flashlight can give you away. If he hears you he will come looking. If he gets angry and you keep making noise, he will get you.

Silence is safety. Stand still with your flashlight off, or hide in a dumpster or locker, and he can't find you.
